Tour Routing Optimization with TSP

Started with a fun question: could Taylor Swift's tour routing have been more efficient? Used Gurobi to solve the Traveling Salesman Problem across her Fearless and Reputation tour stops, with a 1% MIPGap to handle the NP-hard complexity, then compared it against a Nearest Neighbor greedy heuristic. TSP reduced total travel distance by up to 2,774 km — and the route maps made the trade-off between optimality and computation very visual.

Gurobi · Integer Linear Programming · TSP · Nearest Neighbor Heuristic · Python
